Hi! I'm a new faculty at Rice University. I'm looking for PhD students interested in algorithms and theoretical computer science!

Before arriving here, I was a postdoc at MIT and at the Universtiy of Copenhagen. At MIT, and University of Copenhagen I was fortunate to be working with Piotr Indyk , Mikkel Abrahamsen and Mikkel Thorup and their clever students. I completed my PhD at the University of Copenhagen under the supervision of Mikkel Abrahamsen and Mikkel Thorup .

Below you will find an overview of my research interest as well as a (somewhat outdated) list of publications

Research Interests

I'm broadly interested in theoretical computer science. Lately, I've been occupied with problems within differential privacy, learning-augmented algorithms, distribution testing, and hashing. I also enjoy working on various problems within graph theory and computational geometry.
